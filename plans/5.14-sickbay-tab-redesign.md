## 0. Plan summary (revised v2 per Riker split)

**v5.14 ships Stories 1–4 only** — the tab reorganization and the three primitives that need no plug-in cooperation (`<lcars-hr-zones>`, `<lcars-bp-range>`, `<lcars-sleep-score-bar>` — donut replaced per crew C3). Stories 5–6 ride v5.15 — **only after** v5.11 binding-editor and v5.13 Apple Health changes have stabilized AND the HAI plug-in author has committed to the data-contract asks.

**Pre-Story-1 prerequisite work** (Story 0 below) is non-negotiable: amend parent §7.4 redaction selector list, capture bundle-size baseline, ratify open questions Q-A/B/C, write the consent.ecg schema migration, and rebase on the post-v5.11/v5.13 medical-card baseline.

Riker has explicitly blessed this as a supported permanent end state, not "ship and pray."
